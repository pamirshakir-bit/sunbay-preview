./questions

Questions.

Do I need to learn anything?

No. You don’t touch any tools. We send you the posts and you approve them once a week. A message is enough.

How much of my time does it take?

A 20-minute meeting to start. After that, one to two hours for each shoot and one approval a week.

Who can use the photos and videos?

You can, in your own marketing, with no time limit, even if you stop working with us. Videos with library music may stay online where they were posted during our agreement.

Can you promise first place on Google or in AI answers?

No. Nobody can promise that. We do the work and show you the numbers every month.

Let’s talk for 20 minutes.

At your place or by phone, in English, Finnish or French. We bring a plan for your first month. It’s yours to keep.
