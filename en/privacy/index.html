Privacy

How Sun Bay Pictures handles personal data.

Draft: a lawyer checks this page before launch

Last updated 7 November 2026.

Who is responsible

Tmi Pamir Shakir, trading as Sun Bay Pictures, Business ID 3101803-5. Contact: pamir@sunbaypictures.com.

What we collect

  • Emails and calls: your name, contact details and what you tell us.
  • Bookings: your name, email, the time you choose and anything you add.
  • Client details: contact people, billing details, agreements and the material we make for you.
  • Businesses we contact first: the contact details your business publishes itself, on its website, its Google profile or social media.
  • Visits to this site: our hosting service keeps IP addresses and request times in its server logs.

You can read this site without giving us any data. To make you an offer or an agreement we need your contact and billing details.

Why, and on what basis

  • To answer you and prepare an offer: steps before a contract (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)).
  • To do the work we agreed: the contract (Art. 6(1)(b)).
  • To keep in touch with businesses we have met or work with: legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)).
  • To keep accounting records: legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c)).
  • To offer our services to businesses by email or phone: legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)). Every email tells you how to stop them, and one reply is enough.
  • To run this site and keep it safe (server logs): legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)).

Who processes it

  • Google Workspace: email and files.
  • WhatsApp: messages you send us there.
  • Notion: our list of the businesses we talk to.
  • [Hosting provider]: this website and its server logs.
  • [Booking service]: meeting bookings. Today this is Cal.com, through pamirshakir.com.
  • [Analytics provider]: cookieless visit counts, no personal data.

Transfers outside the EU

Some of these services are run by companies in the United States, so your data may be processed there. Those transfers rely on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses.

How long we keep it

Contacts that don’t lead to work: 12 months. Client records: as long as the Finnish Accounting Act requires.

Your rights

You can ask to see your data, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to its use, and receive a copy to take elsewhere. Email pamir@sunbaypictures.com.

You can object to our marketing at any time, and we stop. Where you gave consent, you can withdraw it at any time. We make no automated decisions about you.

Complaints

You can complain to the Finnish Data Protection Ombudsman (tietosuoja.fi). If you live in Belgium, you can also turn to the Belgian Data Protection Authority (dataprotectionauthority.be).

People in our photos and videos

People appear in our photos and videos only with their consent. They can withdraw it at any time by email to pamir@sunbaypictures.com.

Cookies

This site sets no cookies and uses no tracking. Fonts and video are served from this site, not from Google or other outside services.

When you click to another of our pages, one short note goes into your browser’s session storage so the next page can finish the page change. It holds no personal data and is deleted as soon as that page opens.

The profile check runs only in your browser and saves nothing. Your answers reach us only if you send them yourself by WhatsApp or email.
