./how-it-started

How Sun Bay started.

We looked at what was booming in marketing. In the US, agencies were growing fast by doing everything with AI: the posts, the pictures, the replies. It also made every place look like every other place.

We kept the speed and put the people back. The tools sort the data and speed up the boring parts. Pamir comes to your place with a camera, Niko plans the weeks and reads the numbers, and Peres writes the words.

Nothing goes out before you say OK.
